# Phase 37.3 Plan 01: The Exception Family, Its Handler, and the Consuming Transaction Summary

**A raised `AuthRejected` subclass now travels from `auth/creation.py` through the route, the dependency stack and Starlette's MRO dispatch to one handler, and comes out as the byte-identical status and body the client saw before — with the return-value plumbing that used to carry it deleted.**

  `INTERNAL_ERROR` default, a scalars-only `log_fields()`, and the three creation arms

## Decisions Made

### Task 1 — resolved before this executor ran (record this; plans 02 and 04 inherit it)

The developer chose a **fourth option**, not one of the three the plan offered:

> "Remove the ceiling. Keep exceptions in `auth/` but in a separate file."

Consequences, as executed:

1. **D-01 is unchanged and still locked.** The family lives at
   `src/nativespeaker/api/auth/exceptions.py`, inside the measured package, in its own module. The
   relocation option (`option-b`, moving it to `api/errors.py`) was **rejected**. Nothing halted and
   no decision needed amending.
2. **The class ceiling is removed, not widened.** In `tests/unit/test_auth_package_shape.py` the
   `BASELINE` literal, the strictly-smaller-on-every-axis assertion, and the baseline control (which
   existed only to prove that assertion fires, and cannot outlive it) are all deleted. `CURRENT` and
   its case survive, as does the nested-def control that keeps `_measure` honest — the "a later
   phase that grows the package has to come here and write the new number down" property is
   deliberately preserved. The module docstring now records that the ceiling was removed by
   deliberate decision in Phase 37.3, and why: seventeen declarative one-line exception classes
   replace a 44-member enum, three mapping tables and five classifier functions, so the class count
   stopped being a useful complexity proxy for this package.
3. **Log-pipeline question: answered NO.** There are no deployed dashboards or log pipelines, so
   D-02 may retire `auth_rejected`, `create_user_challenge_rejected` and
   `create_user_lookup_rejected` freely. No downstream coordination is required, and no replacement
   events need naming for dashboard-rekeying purposes.

**Plan 04 note:** plan 04 is where the class count would have breached the old ceiling. With the
ceiling gone it simply updates `CURRENT`.

### RESEARCH Open Question 2 — who owns the post-claim consume

**Resolved to D-04's literal wording: `_complete` owns it for raising arms; `create_account` keeps
it for the success path only.** Recorded here so plan 03 does not re-decide. The bare `raise` after
`_consume_and_commit` is safe because after `session.commit()` the session holds no transaction, so
`get_db`'s rollback-on-exception cannot un-consume the handle — the client spent it and must not get
it back. Pinned by `test_the_rejection_leaves_the_consume_to_the_route` (transaction layer),
`TestTheTransactionRejectionIsObservedAtTheHandler` (router layer), and
`test_the_challenge_consumption_committed_despite_the_rollback` (real database).

### RESEARCH Open Question 3 — the two AccountUnavailable arms

One class, one `error_class`, one body. `cause="historical_identity"` / `cause="blocked_user"`
reaches the structured log through `log_fields()` and nowhere else.

### D-03 executed to the letter

The constraint-name helper and both module constants are gone. What survives is the *behaviour*:
the `__cause__` walk sits inline in the `except IntegrityError` arm, still reading the driver's
`constraint_name` attribute rather than the rendered message, still branching three ways, and still
re-raising an unmapped name as a deliberate 500 tripwire.

- **Fix:** Both drivers now perform the route's except-arm choreography (`consume` then `commit`)
  around the raising call, with a comment naming `routers/auth.py::_complete` as the thing being
  mirrored. `test_the_challenge_consumption_committed_despite_the_rollback` and
  `test_both_challenges_were_consumed_and_their_verifiers_cleared` therefore keep their exact
  meaning.
- **Files modified:** `tests/schema/test_create_race.py`, `tests/schema/test_create_atomicity.py`
- **Verification:** both suites pass against the real database.
- **Committed in:** `946b48e`
