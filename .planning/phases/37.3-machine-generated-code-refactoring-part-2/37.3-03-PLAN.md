<objective>
Convert the provider lookup to the exception family — D-17's directive 2.

Purpose: the largest of the four conversions, and the one the precedence suite protects most. The
classifier and the email rule move inline into the read, the result types die at the seam, and the
retry predicate flips from inspecting a returned value to catching a raised marker.

Output: `VerifiedProviderIdentity` at the seam; a `ProviderLookupError` hierarchy in the family;
`_read` raising; `retry_if_exception_type` with an exhaustion callback that raises the unavailable pair;
`_LOOKUP_REJECTIONS` and `_consuming_rejection` gone from the router. <!-- planner-discipline-allow: _LOOKUP_REJECTIONS --> <!-- planner-discipline-allow: _consuming_rejection -->
</objective>
