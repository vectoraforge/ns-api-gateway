<objective>
Promote `tenacity` to a direct dependency in one dependency-scoped commit (D-06), retire `auth/budgets.py` (D-04), and land the single Firebase lookup retry policy that replaces it.

Purpose: D-02 dropped two of the three budget names from §02 step 7, leaving `BudgetGate`'s 150-line multi-name all-or-nothing charge protocol with exactly one name — which degenerates to "have I tried 3 times yet?", i.e. `stop_after_attempt(3)`. Same instinct that retired the hand-rolled `RejectionCounter` in Phase 36 (commit 5f275c8). **Phase 35 D-06 is superseded** by this plan and must be recorded as such.

Output: `tenacity` declared, `uv.lock` regenerated and fully committed, `BudgetGate` gone, and `auth/retry.py` holding the one retry idiom the codebase will use.

**Correcting D-04's phrasing, per RESEARCH Pattern 2 and Pitfall 4.** `FirebaseAdminAdapter.get_user_provider_data` *returns* a `ProviderDataResult`; it never raises. D-04's `retry_if_exception_type` predicate would therefore never fire and the 3-attempt budget would silently become a 1-attempt one. The correct predicate is `retry_if_result`. And result-based exhaustion raises `RetryError` **even with `reraise=True`** — `reraise` re-raises an original *exception*, and there is none — so `retry_error_callback` is MANDATORY, not optional, if D-04's requirement that the `firebase_lookup_unavailable` → `verification_temporarily_unavailable` mapping "be preserved exactly" is to hold.
</objective>
