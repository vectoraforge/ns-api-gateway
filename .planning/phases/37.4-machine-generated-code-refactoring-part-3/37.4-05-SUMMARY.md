## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 1 — Bug in the plan's stated premise] The trimmed lifecycle CHECK is not a tautology

- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The plan, the prior executor and RESEARCH Pitfall 7 all assert that striking the two attempt-id conjuncts leaves `(claimed_at IS NULL AND consumed_at IS NULL) OR (claimed_at IS NOT NULL)`, which is "a tautology". **It is not.** Let A = `claimed_at IS NULL`, B = `consumed_at IS NULL`. The expression is `(A ∧ B) ∨ ¬A`, which reduces to `¬A ∨ B` — false exactly when `claimed_at IS NULL AND consumed_at IS NOT NULL`. `IS NULL` is two-valued in SQL, so no three-valued-logic subtlety rescues the claim.
- **Why it does not change the outcome:** the correct trim and the plan's prescribed rewrite are the *same predicate*. The plan's remedy was right even though its stated reason was wrong, so I performed the rewrite as directed — it states the surviving rule directly instead of leaving a reader to reduce a four-term disjunction.
- **Recorded because:** the phase's threat register rates T-37.4-20 **high** on the premise of vacuousness. The hazard is real (an unthinking trim leaves an unreadable constraint) but its severity was overstated. A future reader should not inherit the incorrect claim.
- **Verification:** truth table above, plus the vacuity negative control, which demonstrates what an actually-vacuous CHECK does.

### 2. [Rule 3 — Blocking] Two files outside the plan's `files_modified` had to change

- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** `tests/unit/test_conflict_classification.py` and `tests/unit/test_create_user_rollback.py` each define a fake store mirroring `consume`'s signature and construct an `AuthChallenge` with `preauth_subject_hash`. Neither is in the plan's file list, and both broke on the signature change.
- **Fix:** dropped `claim_attempt_id` from both fake `consume` signatures, dropped `attempt_id` from both `create_user` calls, and switched both challenge rows to `preauth_subject=SUBJECT`.
- **Committed in:** `5aa7793`

### 4. [Discretion] `TestTheBindingWrittenAtIssuance` was pruned, not deleted whole

- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Plan said:** delete "the class about the binding written at issuance" whole.
- **What I did:** deleted the five methods whose subject the plan removes (the keyring derivation, the key-rotation sensitivity, the no-version-argument AST check, "the raw subject appears nowhere on the row" — which D-11 explicitly gives up — and the no-key-version-column check), replaced them with `test_the_preauth_subject_is_stored_in_plaintext`, and **kept** the four whose subject survives untouched by this phase: the linked identity binds the identity row; a linked identity leaves both pre-auth columns NULL; a pre-auth identity leaves the linked column NULL; the pre-auth issuer is stored in plaintext.
- **Why:** the class's subject — what issuance writes — survives this plan; only its hash-specific members die. Deleting the class whole would have dropped security-relevant binding coverage that this plan does not touch, which is precisely the silent weakening the phase's own warnings are about. No assertion inside a surviving method was deleted.

### 5. [Discretion] Three e2e cases were re-subjected rather than dropped

- `test_the_stored_claim_attempt_id_is_the_winners` → folded into `test_a_second_claim_does_not_change_the_stored_claim_time`, asserting on `claimed_at` instead of the stamped id.
- `test_an_unclaimed_row_is_not_consumable_by_a_null_attempt_id` + `test_a_losing_consume_changes_nothing` → merged into `test_a_consume_without_a_claim_changes_nothing`, keeping the "must not half-apply: neither column moves" assertions.

Two assertions could not be preserved anywhere, because their subject is the deleted column itself: `assert row.claim_attempt_id is None` in `test_an_expired_row_is_left_unclaimed`, and `assert keyring.comparisons == 0` in the precedence file's cleared-binding case.
