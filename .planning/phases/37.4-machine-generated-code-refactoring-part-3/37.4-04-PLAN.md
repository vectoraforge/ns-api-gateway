<objective>
Demote the quota gate to a plain function (D-07) and place the charge at the seam A-03 settled:
`charge_quota()` called from inside `create_chat` and `send_message`, immediately before `ask_llm`.

Purpose: delete the callback chain. `QuotaGate` the class, the `on_admitted` closure wired into the
chat service, the `on_admitted` parameter threaded through the LLM service and the resilience policy,
and the `_AdmissionRejected` wrapper that existed to stop a quota 429 reading as a provider 503 all
go. The property that wrapper protected survives **structurally** rather than by a wrapper, which is
strictly stronger: with the charge outside the retry loop, a quota rejection never enters the
transient-error classifier at all.

Output: `charge_quota` in `quota.py`; a session-factory dependency; two call sites in the chat
service; a resilience layer with no admission callback; and a new `tests/unit/test_quota_seam.py`
pinning both halves of D-07's invariant.

**A-03 settles D-07's "left to planning" clause and overrides D-07's "the endpoint calls" wording.**
The requirement is that quota is a plain function called at a visible point with the callback chain
gone — not that the router literally holds the call. A2 adds zero methods where the alternative needs
two, and satisfies every invariant D-07 states, because all four validations run earlier in the same
method. Do not split `ask_llm`, do not hoist validation into the routers, and do not add `check_*`
methods that re-run the same queries.

**Accepted regression, restated so nobody treats it as a bug:** queue-full (429) and circuit-open
(503) become billable. Today those are raised before admission fires, so a caller hitting a full queue
or an open breaker is not charged; after this plan the charge precedes both. D-07 sanctions this
explicitly.

**Line numbers:** every `file:line` in RESEARCH.md and PATTERNS.md predates plans `37.4-01` through
`37.4-03`. Re-verify line numbers by reading; the facts are sound.
</objective>
