<objective>
Rewrite the prose in the two remaining router modules, the whole `models/` package, and the six top-level service and infrastructure modules (D-13, D-14, D-15, D-16).

Purpose: `routers/auth.py:1-32` is the densest concentration of the phase's anti-patterns in the repo — a 32-line module docstring citing a plan number, a decision id, a spec step, a research pitfall and two more decision ids, arguing with a hypothetical reader ("It looks like a stray commit otherwise — it is not") and instructing a future maintainer ("exactly two fields. Do not add a third"). `models/auth.py` carries per-field comments numbered by ruling. Neither resolves for a reader without the planning directory open.

Output: the request-handling surface and the data model readable cold.

## Pinned definitions

A **multi-line comment block is a run of two or more consecutive own-line comment lines** — the threshold for this phase is two, not three. A surviving note must fit one line within the 120-character limit.

The three commands used verbatim in every acceptance criterion below — **the measurement command**, **the AST-equality check**, and **the cross-reference grep** — are written out in full in the `<objective>` of `.planning/phases/37.1-refactor-machine-generated-code/37.1-03-PLAN.md`. Read that section and use them exactly as written.

## The two poles

**Target style, and the specific model to converge on:** `src/nativespeaker/api/routers/health.py`, `routers/examples.py` and `routers/root.py` all sit at 0% prose because the FastAPI `summary=` and `description=` kwargs carry the user-facing text — that is the right home for route documentation and the shape rewritten `routers/auth.py` should reach. `src/nativespeaker/api/models/chats.py` (0%, 53 lines) is the target shape for `models/auth.py`. `src/nativespeaker/api/logs.py` (2.6%) and `src/nativespeaker/api/database/chats.py` (1.9%) are the register.

**Anti-pattern:** `routers/auth.py:1-32`, and `models/auth.py:100-140` — the "Do not add a state column, and do **not** add an HMAC key-version column" instruction at `:105` plus the ruling-numbered per-field comments at `:130` and `:133`.
