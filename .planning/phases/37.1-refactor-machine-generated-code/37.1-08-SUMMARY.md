## The single deletion

**FOUND-03** — the only requirement removed. It required a route registry placing every route in
exactly one of three categories plus a startup/CI enumeration assertion; `auth/registry.py`, the
assertion and `tests/unit/test_route_registry.py` are all deleted, so nothing survived to amend.
Its checkbox line is gone and a one-line blockquote sits where it was, recording that Phase 37.1
withdrew it, that the fail-closed property is now obtained structurally by
`test_app_wiring.py::TestEveryRouteIsAuthenticated`, and that the genuine loss — the closed
provider-callback category — is flagged forward to phases 43 and 44.

The requirement id appears **exactly once** in the file, in that note. This forced two small
wording choices: FOUND-01's conflict note says "withdrawn just below" rather than naming it, and
the traceability row says "one further requirement deleted outright" — both to keep
`grep -c "FOUND-03"` at 1, as the plan's automated verify requires.
