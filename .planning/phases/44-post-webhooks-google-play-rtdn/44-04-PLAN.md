<objective>
Give the Google path its catalogue and prove, by execution rather than by reading the settings
declaration, that the three deployer variables actually reach the model.

Purpose: `env_nested_delimiter="_"` with `env_nested_max_split=1` is a real trap. If
`GOOGLE_PLAY_PACKAGE_NAME` does not split into the `google_play` field plus a remainder, the config
silently stays at its defaults, `GooglePlayConfig` reads incomplete, and the route answers 503 in
every environment with no error written anywhere. This is cheap to falsify and expensive to
discover.

Output: the `google_play: products:` block in the tracked YAML and the config cases that execute it.
</objective>
