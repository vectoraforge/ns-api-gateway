    **Two corrections to the record, both material.** First, D-11's Google mapping table is written
    with bare state words and counts seven values; Google publishes **nine**, every one prefixed
    `SUBSCRIPTION_STATE_`, and a table keyed on the bare words would have routed every subscriber to

    Pub/Sub acknowledges only 102, 200, 201, 202 and 204 and resends on every other status, so there
    is no 4xx-is-permanent rule. The decision to answer 200 is unchanged and better supported,

    UNIQUE NOT NULL and Phase 45 writes through it, so changing the scheme after rows exist is a data
    migration.

    **Two consequences to record rather than act on.** `SubscriptionStatus.revoked` is Apple-only:
    `subscriptionsv2` exposes no citable revocation signal, so a Google revocation is recorded as
    `expired` with `event_type` 12 preserving the reason (OQ-1). And `linkedPurchaseToken` is parsed
    but not acted on (OQ-3), so after an upgrade or re-signup `core.subscriptions` holds two rows for
    one buyer until Google sends the old token's own expiry notification; the buyer's *grant* is
    correct throughout, because the service supersedes every grant the buyer holds before inserting
    the new one.
