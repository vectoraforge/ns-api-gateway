<objective>
The two store adapters stop being told what time it is, and the boundary cases that needed a pinned
clock move onto the pure helpers that still take one.

`AppStoreNotifications.verify_transaction`, `PlayDeveloperSubscriptions.read` and
`.read_for_restore` each read `datetime.now(UTC)` once and hand that value to `_transaction_status`
or `_status_for`. Those two helpers keep their parameter unchanged — they state a rule, `AGENTS.md`
§ Function shape admits them, and they are where a test can still pin the exact instant at which a
term is over.

**No new function is added under `auth/`.** `tests/unit/test_auth_package_shape.py` carries a
files/classes/functions ratchet, `CURRENT = (8, 24, 67)`, and Phase 49 rewrites it. Adopting RESEARCH
Open Question 5 as written: the phase's one new helper goes in `services/restore.py` (plan 47-05), so
this phase never touches that ratchet.

**Phase boundary.** `PlaySubscriptionSource` is a Protocol that Phase 49 deletes outright. This plan
edits its two method signatures anyway, because until Phase 49 lands the concrete class must keep
conforming to it. Do not delete the Protocol, do not delete
`TestThePushTokenSeamIsTheAnnotation`, and do not touch `TestTheSeamIsTheAnnotation` in the
DeviceCheck tests — all three are Phase 49's.

Purpose: criteria 1, 2 and 3 for the `auth/` package and the Google Play webhook dependency, with the
two store boundaries relocated rather than dropped.
Output: three adapter methods and one Protocol with no datetime parameter, one dependency that
declares none, and the store boundaries proved over the helpers.

**Reversibility:** `reversible` — signatures and their call sites, no stored data and no wire contract.

**Executor note.** `IS_WORKTREE` is **false** in this repository: GSD's detector reports true because
this repo is a submodule whose `.git` is a file, and believing it makes tracking writes vanish.
