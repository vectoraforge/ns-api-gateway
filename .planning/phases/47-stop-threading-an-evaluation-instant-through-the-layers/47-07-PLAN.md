<objective>
The last reader of the dependency goes, then the dependency itself, then the guard that keeps it gone.

`SyncService` is the last service holding an instant. Once it reads its own, `get_evaluated_at` has no
caller and is deleted — which is roadmap criterion 1 in full. This plan then sweeps the remaining
comments and docstrings whose subject is the removed dependency (criterion 4), deletes
`tests/unit/test_sync_clock_capture.py`, which exists only to pin the discipline this phase inverts, and
replaces it with one absence guard covering criteria 1, 2 and 4 in a single module.

**Adopted from RESEARCH Open Question 3, as written:** `tables/grants.py`'s two-sentence comment is cut
to its first sentence, `# The timestamps carry no default.` The first sentence stays true, and it
prevents a real misreading — a reader who adds `default_factory=` to `AccessGrant.starts_at` breaks the
two e2e equality assertions plan 47-03 protects. The second sentence names the shared instant and goes.

**One comment is protected and must survive this sweep:** `crud/grants.py`'s note that a partial index
predicate must be IMMUTABLE, so a database clock cannot appear in
`ix_access_grants_one_active_per_user`. Its subject is the index, not the removed dependency, and it
explains exactly the misreading criterion 3 invites. Every plan in this phase has carried a gate on it.

Purpose: criteria 1, 2 and 4 completed and made permanent by a guard rather than by a claim.
Output: `get_evaluated_at` gone, one deleted test module, one new guard module with its controls.

**Reversibility:** `reversible` — a deletion and a test module, both local.

**Executor note.** `IS_WORKTREE` is **false** in this repository: GSD's detector reports true because
this repo is a submodule whose `.git` is a file, and believing it makes tracking writes vanish.
