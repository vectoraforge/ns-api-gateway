<objective>
The concurrency proof D-12 asks for and Phase 40 D-15 pointed here: two prepared challenges for one
anonymous account, two attempts driven through the production service on independent connections
against real PostgreSQL, meeting at a barrier so that both are known to have observed the same
pre-state.

Purpose: for a first claim there is no grant row, so the `FOR UPDATE` in the activation locks nothing
and the arbiter is the database's two unique indexes rather than the application. That is the one
claim in this phase that cannot be proven by a unit test or an end-to-end case, and it is the claim
that decides whether a simultaneous double claim allocates twice or surfaces as a 500.

Output: the race, and the lock-order proof extended so the new writer cannot introduce a third tier.
</objective>
