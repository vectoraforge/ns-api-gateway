The probe could not classify REBIND-02 and it is **not** auto-resolved. Surfacing it:

> REBIND-02 says these routes write no `audit.auth_events` row ever, and that "rejections keep their
> internal result in the structured security log and increment the bounded-cardinality counter
> metric". The requirement does not say whether "rejections" covers the **quota** rejections this
> phase introduces. It cannot straightforwardly: `record_rejection`'s `result` is typed to
> `AuthEventResult`, a closed 44-value enum the migration says must not gain a spare, and it has no
> quota member. Satisfying REBIND-02 literally for quota rejections would require either a
> non-member string — breaking the bounded-cardinality-by-closed-sets guarantee the counter exists
> to provide — or a forbidden enum widening.
>
> **This plan reads "rejections" as barrier/auth rejections only.** Quota rejections get the
> structured security log (plans 36-03 and 36-04) and no counter, and the no-audit-row half of
> REBIND-02 is proven for them anyway, since it is the half that is unambiguous. **This reading is
> an assumption, not a derivation from the requirement text.** If a reviewer disagrees, the resolution
> is a decision about whether to add a second counter — not a re-plan of these tasks.
