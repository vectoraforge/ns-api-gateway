<objective>
Shrink `core.auth_operation` to its four challenge-bearing values and delete the now-redundant
`core.auth_challenges` operation-membership CHECK, so the issuance handler's membership test can be
`body.operation not in AuthOperation` with no issuable-operation list written anywhere (D-11).

Purpose: the developer's stated concern is one fact duplicated across files — the migration, the
`tables/` mirror and the hardcoded literal in `tests/schema/test_inventory.py` are already three
copies of every enum, and D-11 refuses to add a fourth in the shape of an issuable-operation list.
Shrinking the type makes the type itself the list.
Output: a four-label `core.auth_operation`, a four-member `AuthOperation`, both dev and scratch
databases re-applied from the single migration, and the two schema suites re-baselined — including
the file `40-RESEARCH.md` § P-02 found that `40-CONTEXT.md`'s own fallout list does not name.
</objective>
