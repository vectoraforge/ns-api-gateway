**Record D-09 as a divergence from Apple's production guidance, with its full cost.** Online
certificate revocation checks are off, so verification is pure local computation and no network call
happens on the admission path. The honest cost is larger than a revoked intermediate going unnoticed:
with online checks off, the certificate-validity window is evaluated at the payload's own claimed
signing date, so leaf expiry is not enforced against wall-clock time and an attacker holding an
Apple-issued signing key could keep using it by backdating that date. The decision stands, and the
reason it stands is that the attacker must first hold a leaf key chaining to Apple Root CA G3, which
no ordinary attacker has, and the prize is one wrongly applied sub-$5 subscription.

**Name the obligations that were already dead before this phase existed**, so a reader does not read
them as silently unmet: the backend rate-limit engine and the vendor budgets (Phase 35 D-05), the
gateway limits (Phase 35 D-08, deferred), the `audit.auth_events` row and the operation metadata
(Phase 37.1 D-01 and Phase 38 D-03), the route registry with its `Category`, `RouteMetadata`,
`VERIFIERS` and `NamedVerifier` (Phase 37.1 D-06 and D-10), and the foundation store-verification
interface (Phase 37.2 D-09), which this phase replaced by declaring its seam beside its first
implementation, exactly as FOUND-08's forward flag directs.

**Record the two operational facts and the one accepted residual:**
- Apple retries on 4xx as well as 5xx, five times at 1, 12, 24, 48 and 72 hours, then stops. A
  misconfigured `bundle_id`, `app_apple_id` or `environment` therefore turns every genuine notification
  into a 401 and loses about six days of subscription state silently. The `stage` field on the
  `notification_rejected` warning is what distinguishes that from a forged payload: two of the eight
  verification statuses mean *this deployment is misconfigured* and the rest mean *this payload is not
  Apple's*.
- D-18's product consequence: a buyer's free grant is spent permanently when the paid grant is
  inserted, so a lapsed subscriber holds no grant at all until restore. A consequence, not a divergence.
- The unbounded-request residual: this route is publicly reachable with no credential and no limiter at
  either layer, and each request costs a certificate-path build plus three signature verifications.
  Record it as **accepted, on the Phase 35 D-05 and D-08 precedent**, and say in the entry that it is
  **wider** than the three residuals STATE.md already records under 40 D-22, 41 D-20 and 42 D-16, each
  of which needs a valid token for an existing account, whereas this one needs nothing. Mitigating: the
  work is local and bounded, no session is opened, nothing is written, and the answer is a constant-size
  body — a CPU-burn vector, not an amplification or a data vector. Closes with the v2.1 gateway contract.
