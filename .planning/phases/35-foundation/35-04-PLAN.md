is either dead against the v2.0 schema (the usage and subscription layers, JIT provisioning,

same commit as the routers.

`recorded_assumptions`. The sweep must land as **one atomic commit** or the package is not
importable in between, so it cannot be sliced further. What *was* sliced out is the `§1.4`
context seam it consumes: that is plan 03, which this plan depends on and which has already
shipped `LinkedIdentity`, `get_linked_identity`, and the rest by the time this runs.

Output: an eight-route application, no Firebase or Apple client in the startup path, chat
handlers on the identity context, and a green suite with zero xfail and zero skip.
