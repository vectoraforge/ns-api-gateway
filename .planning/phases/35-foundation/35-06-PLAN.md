<objective>
Complete the barrier: verify the token, resolve `(issuer, subject)` against the database in one
query, enforce the four-outcome admission matrix, attach the typed context, and record every
rejection in telemetry.

Purpose: the tracer proved the reject-and-return mechanics on the wire contract alone. This is
the rest of §1.5 — the part every later phase depends on and none re-implements. It is also
where the anti-oracle guarantee becomes structural: both `account_unavailable` branches leave
the same single query through the same path, so neither does database work the other skips.

distinct identities testable without four Firebase accounts.
