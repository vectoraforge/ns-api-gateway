<objective>
Close VERIFICATION gap 2 (truth 6, 45-REVIEW CR-03): on a move, `write_subscription_grant` expires
every active grant of both accounts, not only the grant for the subscription being moved.

Purpose: `marked_active` holds both accounts' rows because the service locks
`[current_owner, destination]` together. A source account that owns a second, unrelated subscription
loses its grant for that subscription too — a paying customer silently stripped of an entitlement the
restoring caller's proof said nothing about. The write commits cleanly, so nothing surfaces it.
Output: the narrowed set, and the schema case on real PostgreSQL that the phase's 222 passing schema
cases never had.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| the restoring caller -> a third account's rows | The restore writes inside a transaction that holds the locked grant rows of two accounts. Only one of those accounts asked for anything. |
| `services/restore.py` -> `crud/subscriptions.py::write_subscription_grant` | `marked_active` crosses this boundary carrying more rows than the write is entitled to change. The writer, not the caller, decides which of them to end. |

Without it, an authenticated caller ends an unrelated, currently-paid entitlement of an account they have no relationship with, by presenting a proof that names neither. |
