<objective>
Make `POST /auth/create-user` a completion-only endpoint: delete the mode signal, the query-string
accessor that fed it, the loosely-typed field that existed to force a chosen status code, and the
racy already-linked pre-check.

Purpose: with challenge issuance living on its own route, the partition the mode signal existed to
disambiguate no longer exists. Every mechanism built to resolve that ambiguity is now machinery
guarding a state that cannot occur.

Output: one endpoint per phase of the operation, a body validated by the framework, and the database
constraint as the sole already-linked authority.
</objective>
