## Decisions Made

**Flat model, never a discriminated-union root.** The todo originally proposed binding the three response models as a union. That was deliberately not built. The strict conversion rewrites an object by forcing every property into `required` and setting `additionalProperties` false, but it does **not** descend into a root-level `oneOf` — a union root would have shipped looking strict while leaving every branch, and the nested `Issue`, unconstrained. This is the single most likely way the change fails without failing loudly, so it is guarded three ways: by construction, by a `grep -rn 'RootModel' src` that must return nothing, and by a unit case pinning the root as a flat `object` with no `oneOf`/`anyOf`.

**The chain still yields a plain dict.** A trailing `model_dump()` step keeps `ChatService.ask_llm`'s `resolved_mode` dispatch and its re-validation working unchanged. Deliberate, not an oversight.

**Post-hoc re-validation in `ChatService.ask_llm` stays** (the todo's first open question). `strict` travels in provider-specific keyword arguments — every wrapper accepts it, only some enforce it — so the guarantee is a property of the provider and model in use, not of our code. A second in-process check is nearly free and is the only part we own outright.

**The empty-list defaults on `AnalyzeResponse` stay** (the todo's second open question). Under a strict schema they no longer do provider-boundary work, because the strict rewrite marks every declared field required regardless of a Pydantic default. They stay because they stopped being a workaround and became the client-facing contract, and because they remain the fallback on any future model that accepts `strict` without honouring it.

**The e2e gate asserts on the raw payload, not the parsed model.** A parsed `ChatModelResponse` would have its list defaults filled in and would mask exactly the omission the gate exists to catch. `include_raw=True` returns `{"raw", "parsed", "parsing_error"}`; the assertion reads the provider's own JSON out of `raw`.

**No provider key means skip, never mock.** A mocked chain returns whatever it was told to return and proves nothing about whether the provider honoured the constraint.

**Archived to `.planning/todos/done/`.** No `done/` or `archived/` sibling existed anywhere in the repo, and none is prescribed by GSD; `pending/` plus a front matter of `status: done` implies `done/`.
