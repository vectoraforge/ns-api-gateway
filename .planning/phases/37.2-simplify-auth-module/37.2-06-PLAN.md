<objective>
Merge the modules that serve one flow into that flow's module, fold the remaining single-caller
indirections into their call sites, and put a machine-checked assertion under the package's size so
success criterion 5 is measured rather than asserted in prose.

Purpose: the `auth/` package carried fourteen files for one create-user feature. After this phase's
deletions the sprawl that remains is organizational, not functional — three modules exist to hold two
pure functions, a retry policy, and a single log line.

Output: a smaller package whose file count is a fact a test can check, with every deletion justified
as carrying no behavior.
</objective>
