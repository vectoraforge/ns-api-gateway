<objective>
Turn two of this phase's structural claims into executable checks: that exactly one place in the tree
can create a registered account grant, and that no vendor call can end up inside the writer or under
a held lock.

Purpose: REGGRANT-01 is a negative property over the whole source tree and REGGRANT-02's
no-network-under-a-lock half is a negative property over one function. Neither can be observed from
a passing happy path, and both are exactly the kind of guard that passes vacuously if written
carelessly — which is why each one here is mutation-tested before it is trusted.

Output: a registered sibling of the single-writer walk with its own mutation control, and an extended
ordering module covering the registered claim.
</objective>
